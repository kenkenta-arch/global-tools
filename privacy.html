Privacy

Tool inputs

Any text, numbers, or files you enter in a NimbleDocket tool are processed on your device and never sent to us or to another service. NimbleDocket currently sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics scripts.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical information such as your IP address when you load a page. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

Future advertising

If ads are added in the future, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. Advertising vendors may also use web beacons or IP addresses for ad delivery and measurement.

You can opt out of Google’s personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. For participating third-party vendors, visit the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Disclaimer

Tool results are for general information and may contain errors. Verify results before relying on them for important decisions. The tools do not provide professional advice.
